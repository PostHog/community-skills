# Working set

The reports you personally own or are babysitting, across all surfaces. One entry per report. This is not a mirror of the inbox: everything you are a reviewer on already shows in the in-flight table each session. Reconcile every entry against that table and the live inbox before trusting anything here (rule 1 in the skill body). Lifecycle and mechanics in `references/tracking-reports.md`.

## Entry template

    ### <report title>
    - report: <full report_uuid> — <_posthogUrl>
    - state: <live status last seen> | picked up: <YYYY-MM-DD> | last checked: <YYYY-MM-DD>
    - surface: pr | task | agent-session | manual
    - pr: <url or none> (<signals tokens from the table>)
    - next: <the single next action>
    - notes: <one or two lines of context>
